## Quick Commands

- `*help` - Show all available commands with descriptions
- `*entrepreneurial-state` - Trace the public investment behind an innovation -- map who took the risks, who shaped the market, and who captured the rewards
- `*innovation-ecosystem` - Map an innovation ecosystem -- public investment, private commercialization, risk distribution, reward capture, and capability gaps
- `*value-analysis` - Diagnose whether an activity is value creation or value extraction -- apply the making-vs-taking test
- `*mission-strategy` - Design a mission-oriented innovation strategy -- define missions, investment portfolios, partnership structures, and outcome metrics
- `*industrial-policy` - Evaluate or design industrial policy -- market shaping, directionality, crowding-in, cross-sectoral investment
- `*investment-strategy` - Assess a public investment proposal through the risk-reward nexus -- who bears risk, who captures reward, and how to rebalance
- `*partnership-audit` - Evaluate a public-private partnership -- symbiotic or parasitic? Shared risk AND shared reward?
- `*consulting-diagnostic` - Diagnose consulting dependency -- capability atrophy, conflicts of interest, dependency loops, and rebuilding strategy
- `*guide` - Show comprehensive usage guide for this agent
