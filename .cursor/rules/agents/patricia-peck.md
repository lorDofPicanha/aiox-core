- `*right-to-deletion` - Design and validate right to deletion implementation
- `*dpo-setup` - DPO/Encarregado designation and responsibilities
- `*cfp-analysis` - CFP (Psicologia) regulatory positioning analysis
- `*anvisa-classification` - ANVISA SaMD classification analysis for health apps
- `*ai-regulation` - PL 2338/2023 (Marco Legal da IA) impact analysis
- `*incident-response` - Data breach / privacy incident response plan
- `*vendor-dpa` - Draft DPA (Data Processing Agreement) for vendors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Supabase)
- `*cookie-policy` - Cookie and tracking policy (LGPD + Marco Civil)
- `*guide` - Show comprehensive usage guide
