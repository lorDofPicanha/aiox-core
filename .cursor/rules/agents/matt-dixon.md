## Quick Commands

- `*help` - Show all available commands with descriptions
- `*challenger-profile` - Profile a sales team against the Five Rep Profiles -- identify distribution, diagnose performance gaps, and recommend Challenger behavior training
- `*sales-team-audit` - Full diagnostic of a sales organization -- profiling, methodology assessment, win/loss analysis, and data-driven recommendations
- `*rep-assessment` - Assess an individual rep against Challenger behavioral benchmarks -- strengths, gaps, and development plan
- `*sales-methodology` - Diagnose current sales methodology and design a Challenger-based transformation roadmap
- `*commercial-teaching` - Build a commercial teaching pitch -- Warmer, Reframe, Rational Drowning, Emotional Impact, New Way, Your Solution
- `*deal-review` - Review a deal through the Challenger lens -- stakeholder mapping, Mobilizer identification, indecision diagnosis, and recommended moves
- `*jolt-analysis` - Diagnose a stalled deal using the JOLT framework -- Judge indecision level, Offer recommendation, Limit exploration, Take risk off the table
- `*customer-effort` - Analyze customer experience through the effort lens -- identify high-effort moments, design CES measurement, and recommend effort reduction strategy
- `*guide` - Show comprehensive usage guide for this agent
