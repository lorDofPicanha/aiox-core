Why Bretda

A table you play on for a night.
An object you keep for a life.

Most game tables are bought. A Bretda is commissioned. The frame is shaped from a single run of selected hardwood, its corners eased to a soft 4 mm radius so light travels along them rather than catches. The base is a continuous ribbon of polished steel — no visible fasteners, no seams to forgive.

We accept a limited number of commissions each year. Every table is assigned to a single maker who signs the underside of the frame, then crated, insured and delivered to your home — installed and levelled by a specialist, not left in a box at your door. The wait is the proof of the work.
