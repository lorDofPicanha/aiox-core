## THE CASSINA PERSPECTIVE 2026

The **2026 collection** is a comprehensive narrative of eclectic design, presented with a focus on the concept of material intelligence. This year, highlights include Patricia Urquiola’s sofa system, designs by Chiara Andreatti, Formafantasma, Gaetano Pesce and Philippe Starck, as well as the Karakter x Cassina pieces by Aldo Bakker, Gianfranco Frattini, Verner Panton and Nicos Zographos. Also showcased are the **2026 Outdoor Collection** and new lighting designs by Charles & Ray Eames, Angelo Mangiarotti, Neri&Hu and Linde Freya Tangelder.
