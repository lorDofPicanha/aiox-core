# Official Media Audit - Batch 01

Date: 2026-07-12

Purpose: determine whether publicly available, owner-originated brand media is sufficient for a real proposal prototype. An asset is eligible only when it is published on an official channel controlled by the business or supplied by the owner. Photos from review platforms, maps, travel sites, or directories are not eligible for reuse.

## Outcome

None of the five prospects passes the evidence gate yet. The public web confirms the official channels and, for several businesses, enough *possible* content exists. However, anonymous access to Instagram did not expose eight verifiable media permalinks or source-image URLs. This report deliberately does not relabel third-party gallery photos as owner assets.

| Dora Marie | HOLD | Official identity is established, but the current Instagram handle and eight attributable media links were not independently captured. |
| Vanessa Hayashi Doceria | HOLD | The official Instagram is established and historical records indicate a large post library, but no eight public asset permalinks were captured. |

## Required Collection Step

The only compliant path to convert any of these records to PASS is to use the business's official account while logged in or obtain a media folder from the owner. Record eight or more media permalinks, source URL, media type, depicted product/context, capture date, and reuse approval. That avoids presenting customer uploads or directory images as the business's own work.
