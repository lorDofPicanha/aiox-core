# Research Plan v1 — Confectionery Prospect Mockups

**Story:** SP-001  
**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Decision owner:** Breno Cerqueira  
**Status:** Gate A approved through the written specification

## Real Question

Which three confectionery businesses inside Blumenau combine a material digital
gap, current commercial vitality, enough traceable first-party media, and a
plausible path from a better web journey to more qualified orders or inquiries?

## Pending Decision

Freeze exactly three prospects and choose a distinct, evidence-led conversion
and visual direction for each private sales mockup.

The cost of a wrong decision is wasted bespoke production time and a weak sales
conversation. The decision is reversible until a prospect pack is built; no
external outreach is authorized by this story.

## Non-negotiable Constraints

- Blumenau city limits and the same confectionery/dessert niche.
- Website-gap score of at least `25/40` before total-score evaluation.
- At least eight individually traceable first-party media assets plus logo or
  wordmark evidence before mockup production.
- No generated, stock, reviewer, map, or directory media presented as the
  prospect's property.
- Facts without public evidence use `unknown_not_public` and stay out of the
  rendered page.
- No benchmark clone, external send, deployment, purchase, or publication.
- Sequence: shared layer → P1 → L1 → P2 → L2 → P3.

## Initial Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Initial confidence | Falsifier |
|---|---:|---|
| Instagram/catalog-first businesses have an institutional trust and discovery gap | 70% | Their current journey already resolves search, trust and high-intent action |
| Public first-party media is enough for three premium mockups | 55% | Fewer than three eligible candidates clear the 8+logo media gate |
| Occasion-led architecture is more valuable than a generic product gallery | 80% | Public offers show no meaningful occasion, gifting, event or order segmentation |
| A value thesis improves prospecting quality over a visual reveal alone | 80% | Human outreach later shows no difference in qualified response |
| Three distinct compositions can share invisible primitives without looking templated | 75% | Anti-clone or cross-prospect comparison exceeds approved thresholds |

## Active Dimensions

1. **Market/business:** candidate vitality, customer jobs, current alternatives,
   conversion paths, commercial occasions, and scenario-based value.
2. **Technical/design:** responsive behavior, accessibility, performance,
   extractability, design tokens, and working standalone mockups.
3. **Provenance/privacy:** ownership signals, allowed private use, factual
   traceability, and exclusion of reviewer/directory media.

## Master Questions

1. Does the candidate lack an effective owned institutional website today?
2. Is the business demonstrably active and commercially relevant now?
3. Can eight first-party assets plus brand identity be traced individually?
4. Which customer jobs are fragmented or hidden in the current journey?
5. Which patterns recur across strong same-niche websites, and which are merely
   decorative fashions?
6. Which prospect-specific signals make the result unmistakably theirs?
7. Which action and post-launch metric correspond to each page section?
8. What evidence would invalidate the candidate or design direction?

## Source Scoring

Market/business sources use relevance `50%`, recency `30%`, authority `20%`.
Technical/design sources use relevance `50%`, recency `35%`, authority `15%`.
Sources below `3/5` are discovery/background only. Decision claims require three
independent sources or an explicit divergence/provisional label.

Priority order: official business source → official registry/local government →
current independent review platform → reputable directory corroboration →
search snippet as discovery pointer only.

## Stop Criteria

- Five consecutive sources add no material claim to a sub-question; or
- The approved evidence quota is met; or
- Shared research reaches 4 hours, one prospect reaches 10 hours, or the batch
  reaches 34 hours.

An unresolved critical, reducible uncertainty receives one surgical research
cycle. Irreducible uncertainty becomes a proposed human experiment, not more
desk research.

## Deliverables and Consumers

- `benchmark-index.md` → brand and UX direction.
- `conversion-patterns.md` → page narrative and CTA architecture.
- `anti-clone-matrix.md` → independent visual review.
- Candidate scores and evidence manifests → freeze/replacement decision.
- Per-prospect `DESIGN.md` and value thesis → frontend implementation.
- QA and adversarial reports → Breno's private outreach decision.

## Research Risks

- Anonymous Instagram access can reveal a channel but not enough exact media
  permalinks; such candidates remain `HOLD`.
- Third-party listings can contain stale NAP data and customer-owned images.
- Static design extraction can fail on JavaScript shells or LLM synthesis.
- Current site quality cannot be inferred from age or visual taste alone; the
  deterministic website-gap tree controls eligibility.
