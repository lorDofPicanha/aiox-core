# Bananas Gostosas — Design System

Brand: Bananas Gostosas, pioneiros na arte da banana passa em Corupa/SC. Family heritage food maker ("Uma Doce Tradicao"), Indicacao Geografica de Corupa. Natural, wholesome, folksy, joyful.
Tagline: "Uma Doce Tradicao" / "Pioneiros na arte da banana passa".
Mascot: a cheerful girl with bananas braided into her hair. Warm and homey ("casa de vo").

## Colors
- banana-yellow: #F6CB1B (primary — packaging, highlights, energy)
- yellow-deep: #E4A400 (hover, depth, ripe banana)
- brown-900: #3E2A1B (wordmark and body text — chocolate/coffee brown)
- brown-700: #6B4A2E (secondary text)
- leaf-green: #4E8A3D (natural/leaf accent, from banana-leaf packaging art)
- cream: #FBF3DE (soft background, warmth)
- white: #FFFFFF
Body text is brown-900 on cream/white (high contrast). Text on banana-yellow is always brown-900 (never white — yellow+white fails contrast).

## Typography
- Display / headings: a warm, characterful face with personality (e.g. Fraunces, or a friendly rounded slab). Should feel handmade and homey, echoing the chunky rounded wordmark — NOT cold or corporate.
- Body: readable, friendly humanist sans, minimum 18px, line-height 1.5 (e.g. Nunito Sans, Mulish).
- Numbers/prices: same sans, bold.

## Shape and motion
- Generous rounding: cards 20px, pills 999px (friendly, soft, wholesome).
- Warm soft shadows, never cold blue.
- Organic touches welcome (leaf shapes, gentle curves). Subtle motion; honor prefers-reduced-motion; no autoplay carousels.

## Art direction
- Bright, natural, appetizing product photography (banana passa, potes, packaging) on cream/white.
- Warmth and family: heritage, tradition, Corupa, "casa de vo", handmade.
- Health cues: 100% natural, sem acucar, sem gluten, rico em fibras (real product claims).
- Mood: joyful, trustworthy, natural, nostalgic.

## Voice
Warm, homey, first person, affectionate. Portuguese (Brazil). Real copy from feed: "Tradicao, sabor e carinho em cada mordida", "um jeito pratico e nutritivo".
