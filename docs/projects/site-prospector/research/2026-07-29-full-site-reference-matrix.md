## Required target sitemap

Every business receives at least:

1. `index.html` — complete homepage with product discovery.
2. `catalogo.html` — full catalogue with filters/category controls.
3. `categoria.html` — one complete collection/category landing page.
4. `produto.html` — detailed product screen with gallery and primary action.
5. `sobre.html` — brand story and process.
6. `contato.html` — service/contact architecture with unknown facts omitted.
7. `carrinho.html` — cart/order-review concept.
8. `guia.html` — niche-specific guide.

The guide is adapted by niche:

- fashion: fit and measurements;
- accessories/jewelry: materials and care;
- gourmet: flavours, tasting, and conservation;
- home/textiles: dimensions, materials, and care;
- aroma/cosmetics: notes, ingredients, and ritual.

## Non-negotiable gates

- Product, category, and catalogue pages must be visually denser and more
  commercially useful than the homepage.
- Each niche family must have its own information architecture and interaction
  vocabulary.
- Each business must have a distinct composition, palette, type hierarchy,
  crop language, and voice.
- No unsupported price, inventory, delivery promise, material, address,
  testimonial, or contact detail.
- Only inspected first-party target images.
- Every screen must visibly say `Conceito visual não oficial`.
- Desktop 1440px and mobile 390px captures for every page.
- No dead navigation, horizontal overflow, publication, deploy, outreach,
  external send, ad spend, or git push.
