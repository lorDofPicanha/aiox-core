## Extracted Principles and Decision Mapping

### April Dunford — positioning

Principles used:

- Start from the customer's real competitive alternatives.
- Map differentiated attributes to customer value and best-fit customers.
- Choose a market frame that makes the value obvious.
- Carry positioning coherently through story and pitch.

Application:

- The competitive alternative is dependence on Instagram, ordering hubs, and
  fragmented discovery—not merely “having no website.”
- Candidate scoring favors businesses with a visible, evidenced value gap.
- Each private pack must align current alternative → differentiated web
  mechanism → commercial value → prospecting story.

### Oli Gardner — conversion

Principles used:

- Give the page one purpose and a dominant action.
- Preserve message match between promise, content, and CTA.
- Minimize friction and distractions.
- Use specific, real social proof rather than invented reassurance.

Application:

- Every mockup declares one primary conversion goal and one primary CTA label.
- Each material section maps pain, mechanism, action, and measurable outcome.
- Review themes remain research evidence unless explicitly cleared for display.
- Generic filler and unsupported trust claims fail the Conversion/Evidence Gates.

### Brad Frost — design systems

Principles used:

- Design systems govern reusable systems, not cloned pages.
- Tokens and primitives establish coherence.
- Higher-order compositions should express the product or brand context.

Application:

- Shared implementation is limited to invisible primitives, research methods,
  accessibility rules, and quality gates.
- Brand tokens carry provenance and confidence from prospect evidence.
- Organisms, templates, content, and page composition remain unique per prospect.
- The anti-clone matrix compares every result with each benchmark.

## Result

The consultation directly informed:

1. The deterministic candidate-selection rubric and best-fit threshold.
2. The one-goal/one-primary-CTA conversion gate.
3. The hybrid boundary between shared primitives and bespoke compositions.
4. The requirement for traceable proof and a value thesis before visual reveal.
5. The anti-clone matrix and sequential prospect checkpoints.

No external outreach, publication, purchase, deployment, or push was performed.
