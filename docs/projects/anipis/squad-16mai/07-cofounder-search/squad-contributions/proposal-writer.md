## 2. Common Pitfalls — 5 Errors That Destroy Co-Founder JD Pipelines

These are the failures I have personally seen in 100+ founder/CXO recruitment documents. Each is mapped to a v1.1 mitigation.

1. **Burying equity in the footer / equity ambiguity.** Senior candidates filter on equity before they read responsibilities. JDs that say "competitive equity" or hide the band in a paragraph at the end lose 60-70% of qualified applicants in the first 15 seconds. *Mitigation in v1.1:* equity range surfaced in the header table; full banding logic in §5 with explicit qualifications-to-percentage mapping.

2. **"Wear many hats" language without veto rights.** Founders write JDs that romanticize ambiguity ("you'll do a bit of everything") without codifying decision authority. Senior clinical hires read this as *"you'll be blamed for everything but decide nothing."* Pipeline collapses. *Mitigation in v1.1:* §5 decision-rights table with explicit veto allocations + deadlock-breaker clause.

3. **Vesting cliff weaponization.** JDs that mention "4-year vest" but omit cliff terms, acceleration, and trial-period treatment create distrust before the first call. Sophisticated candidates (and their lawyers) assume the worst. *Mitigation in v1.1:* §5 explicitly states 1y cliff, single/double-trigger acceleration, 30-day trial vesting math (1/48), and pari-passu dilution.

4. **Outcome metrics absent or vanity-stated.** "Be a great clinical leader" is not an outcome. Without 6/12/24-month success milestones, the candidate cannot self-assess fit and the founder cannot fire fairly. JDs missing this are a leading indicator of post-hire conflict. *Mitigation in v1.1:* §6 enumerates 6m/12m/24m milestones with quantified targets (MAUs, retention %, PHQ-9 delta, RCT recruitment %).

5. **"Not a fit if…" framed as gatekeeping rather than mutual time-saving.** v0 used the "NOT a fit if" list well; the risk is when JDs use it to posture ("you better be hardcore"). Pipeline reads it as toxic. *Mitigation in v1.1:* §4 reframed as *"To save you and us time"* — same content, buyer-respectful tone.

## 3. Legal Review Checklist (OAB-SP labor + corporate equity)

Send this checklist with the JD to the reviewing attorney. Ten items, each tied to a defensible Brazilian legal position. Do **not** publish until items 1-7 are signed off; items 8-10 may run in parallel.

1. **Equity grant structure (PJ vs. CLT vs. autônomo).** Confirm grant vehicle. Brazilian common-stock founder grants typically issued via *acordo de sócios* with vesting suspensive conditions; CLT employment converts to LTDA/SA quotas at conversion. Determine whether candidate will be admitted as quotista from day 1 or hold contractual vesting rights only.

2. **Vesting cliff enforceability.** Confirm 1-year cliff with reverse-vesting buyback rights at original-issue price (R$0.01/quota or similar nominal) is enforceable under current Brazilian corporate-law jurisprudence. Anchor in *acordo de sócios* with specific performance clause.

3. **Single-trigger vs. double-trigger acceleration.** Validate that 100% single-trigger acceleration in first 24 months does not create securities-law triggers under CVM Resolução 88 (crowdfunding) or future Series A negotiation friction. Standard US single-trigger language requires BR translation.

4. **Intellectual property assignment (clinical content, prompts, RCT protocol).** Critical. All clinical content (system prompts, banned-phrases, crisis flows, training corpus, RCT protocol drafts) must assign to Anipis legal entity in perpetuity, irrevocably. Include "moral rights" waiver (CRP/CRM signatory retains professional accountability without retaining commercial IP). Reference Lei 9.610/98 art. 49.

5. **Non-compete (cláusula de não-concorrência).** Define scope: digital mental-health BR, 24-month post-departure, with compensation (Brazilian labor jurisprudence requires consideration). Geography: Brazil only. Carve-outs: academic publication, individual clinical practice not in B2B/B2C tech mental-health.

6. **Non-solicit (clientes + employees).** 24-month post-departure; scope = Anipis employees, advisors, committee members, beta cohort, RCT participants, B2B prospects.

7. **Confidentiality + NDA scope.** Pre-LOI NDA covering 9MB dossier; post-LOI confidentiality covering trade secrets indefinitely. Carve-out for required regulatory disclosure (CFM, ANVISA, MP).

8. **Severance (rescisão sem justa causa) terms.** Define: if Founder-CEO terminates without cause pre-Series A, what equity is retained beyond vested? Common: 3-6 months acceleration as severance. Brazilian CLT severance interacts with PJ contractor model — clarify which applies.

9. **Conflict-of-interest disclosure obligation.** Mandatory disclosure of: current advisory roles at competitors (Cíngulo/Zenklub/Vittude/Conexa/TelaVita), current consultancy, board seats, RCT investigator roles at other studies. Annual update required.

10. **CFM/CFP regulatory liability allocation.** Critical and underexplored in v0. Allocate professional liability for clinical-signatory actions: (a) Anipis maintains seguro de responsabilidade profissional ≥R$2M; (b) personal liability not waived (Brazilian professional councils do not permit waiver); (c) indemnification clause for actions taken in good faith following committee/protocol procedures. OAB-SP labor partner should consult OAB-SP health-law specialist (e.g., Patricia Peck) on this item specifically.
