## Quick Commands

- `*help` - Show all available commands with descriptions
- `*story-structure` - Design a complete story structure using the five-part architecture -- inciting incident, progressive complications, crisis, climax, resolution
- `*story-review` - Structural diagnosis of a narrative -- find missing parts, dead scenes, value failures, and Gap problems
- `*storynomics-strategy` - Transform business communication from claims and features into story-driven narrative using Storynomics principles
- `*pitch-design` - Design a pitch or presentation as a story with inciting incident, progressive complications, crisis, and climax
- `*dialogue-craft` - Analyze and improve dialogue for subtext, verbal action, character voice, and dramatic power
- `*case-study-narrative` - Transform raw case study data into a compelling narrative with story structure
- `*controlling-idea` - Define or discover the Controlling Idea of a narrative -- one sentence expressing how and why life changes
- `*guide` - Show comprehensive usage guide for this agent
