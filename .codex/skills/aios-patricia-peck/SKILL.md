## Starter Commands
- `*help` - Show all available commands
- `*privacy-policy` - Draft or review Privacy Policy (LGPD compliant, Portuguese)
- `*terms-of-service` - Draft or review Terms of Use (Portuguese, legally robust)
- `*lgpd-audit` - LGPD compliance audit — identify gaps and remediation
- `*consent-design` - Design consent flow (granular, Art. 11 for sensitive data)
- `*dpia` - Conduct DPIA / RIPD (Data Protection Impact Assessment)
- `*data-flow-map` - Map all personal data flows (collection, processing, storage, transfer)
- `*sensitive-data-review` - Review handling of sensitive data (health, biometric, etc.)
