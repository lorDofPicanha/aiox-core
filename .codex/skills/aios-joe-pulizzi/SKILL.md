## Starter Commands
- `*help` - Show all available commands with descriptions
- `*content-inc` - Apply the Content Inc. 7-step model — sweet spot, content tilt, build base, harvest, diversify, monetize, exit
- `*content-tilt` - Discover your content tilt — the unique angle where no competition exists
- `*content-mission` - Create a content mission statement — WHO you serve, WHAT you deliver, WHY they care
- `*content-audit` - Audit existing content strategy — tilt assessment, channel focus, publishing cadence, audience ownership, monetization readiness
- `*content-review` - Review content materials through Pulizzi's lens — editorial quality, audience fit, tilt presence, mission alignment
- `*ownership-audit` - Audit owned vs. rented audience — email list health, platform dependence, conversion strategy
- `*newsletter-strategy` - Design a newsletter-first audience strategy — value proposition, cadence, subscriber conversion, monetization path
