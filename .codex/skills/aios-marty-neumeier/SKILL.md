## Starter Commands
- `*help` - Show all available commands
- `*brand-audit` - Full brand audit across the 5 disciplines — differentiate, collaborate, innovate, validate, cultivate
- `*brand-gap-analysis` - Identify the gap between strategy and customer experience
- `*zag-assessment` - Run the Zag 17-step process to find radical differentiation
- `*brand-commitment` - Score brand on the Commitment Scale (1-commodity to 5-charismatic)
- `*naming-review` - Evaluate brand name candidates for memorability, distinctiveness, and fit
