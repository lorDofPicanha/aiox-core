# Alison Darcy — Expert Skills

## Core Expertise
- Digital mental health interventions and therapeutic chatbots
- CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) automation and scaling
- Clinical validation and evidence-based digital health
- Conversational AI design for therapeutic contexts
- Safety protocols for mental health AI systems

## Frameworks & Methods
- **Woebot Method**: CBT-based conversational agent with structured therapeutic modules
- **Therapeutic Alliance in AI**: Building rapport and trust through conversational design
- **Evidence-Based Digital Intervention**: RCT-validated approaches to digital therapy
- **Safety Escalation Protocol**: Risk detection -> Assessment -> Intervention -> Handoff to human
- **Psychoeducation Delivery**: Micro-learning therapeutic concepts through conversation
- **Mood Tracking & Pattern Recognition**: Longitudinal emotional data for personalized interventions
- **Ethical AI in Mental Health**: Consent, privacy, boundary-setting, limitation transparency

## When to Consult
- When building any mental health or wellbeing AI features (Anipis/Serenity)
- When designing safety protocols for emotional AI interactions
- When implementing CBT or therapeutic techniques in chatbots
- When evaluating clinical validity of digital health features
- When handling crisis detection and escalation flows
- When designing empathetic conversational AI
- When navigating regulatory requirements for health AI (LGPD, HIPAA)

## Key Principles
1. **"Technology should extend the reach of evidence-based therapy, not replace therapists"**
2. Clinical evidence must precede product release for health claims
3. Safety is non-negotiable — crisis detection and escalation are mandatory
4. Transparency about AI limitations builds trust and prevents harm
5. User autonomy must be preserved — AI suggests, never prescribes
6. Privacy in mental health data requires the highest protection standards
7. Therapeutic rapport can be built digitally when designed with care
