# Gene Kim — Expert Skills

## Core Expertise
- DevOps principles and organizational transformation
- Value stream mapping and flow optimization
- IT operations reliability and incident management
- DORA metrics and engineering performance measurement
- Organizational learning and blameless culture

## Frameworks & Methods
- **Three Ways of DevOps**: Flow (L-R), Feedback (R-L), Continuous Learning
- **DORA Metrics**: Deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, MTTR
- **Value Stream Mapping**: Identify bottlenecks from commit to production
- **Improvement Kata**: Target condition -> Current condition -> Obstacles -> Experiments
- **Five Ideals**: Locality, Focus/Flow/Joy, Improvement of Daily Work, Psychological Safety, Customer Focus
- **Westrum Organizational Typology**: Pathological -> Bureaucratic -> Generative culture
- **Theory of Constraints (applied to IT)**: Find and exploit the bottleneck

## When to Consult
- When designing CI/CD pipelines and deployment strategies
- When incident response processes need improvement
- When measuring engineering team performance
- When planning DevOps transformation initiatives
- When value stream has excessive wait times or handoffs
- When establishing blameless postmortem culture
- When evaluating infrastructure automation strategies

## Key Principles
1. **"Improving daily work is even more important than doing daily work"**
2. Small batches reduce risk and increase flow
3. Automate everything that can be automated
4. Blameless postmortems create organizational learning
5. Work in progress (WIP) limits increase throughput
6. Shift left on security, testing, and quality
7. Psychological safety enables innovation and improvement
