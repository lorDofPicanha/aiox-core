# Alex Hormozi — Expert Skills

## Frameworks & Methods
- **Value Equation**: Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice)
- **Grand Slam Offer**: Stack of solutions that make the offer irresistible
- **Lead Magnet Framework**: Solve a narrow problem completely for free to acquire leads
- **Price-to-Value Discrepancy**: Charge based on value delivered, not cost or time
- **Offer Stack Building**: Core offer + bonuses + guarantee + urgency + naming
- **100M Leads Framework**: Content, outreach, ads, affiliates — the 4 acquisition channels
- **Brick-by-Brick Method**: List every obstacle, then create a solution for each one

## When to Consult
- When creating or restructuring a product/service offer
- When pricing feels wrong (too cheap or not converting)
- When lead generation is stalling or CAC is too high
- When designing landing pages or sales funnels
- When adding bonuses, guarantees, or urgency elements
- When planning business model for new SaaS/product
- When evaluating monetization strategy for any project

## Key Principles
1. **"Make people an offer so good they would feel stupid saying no"**
2. Price on value, not on cost — charge what it's worth to them
3. Guarantees reduce perceived risk and increase conversion
4. Scarcity and urgency must be real, never fabricated
5. Solve every objection before they think of it
6. Volume negates luck — more offers, more leads, more wins
7. The best marketing is a great product with great delivery
