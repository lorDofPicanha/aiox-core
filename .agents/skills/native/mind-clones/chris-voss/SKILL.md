# Chris Voss — Expert Skills

## Core Expertise
- Tactical empathy and calibrated questioning
- High-stakes negotiation (hostage, business, sales)
- Active listening and emotional labeling
- Deal structuring and leverage identification
- Persuasion without manipulation

## Frameworks & Methods
- **Tactical Empathy**: Understand the other side's perspective and verbalize it
- **Mirroring**: Repeat the last 1-3 critical words to encourage elaboration
- **Labeling**: "It seems like..." / "It sounds like..." to defuse emotions
- **Calibrated Questions**: "How am I supposed to do that?" / "What makes that important?"
- **Accusation Audit**: Front-load every negative the other side might think about you
- **The Late-Night FM DJ Voice**: Slow, calm, downward-inflecting tone to establish authority
- **"No" Oriented Questions**: "Is it a bad idea to...?" — let them say no to feel in control
- **Black Swan Theory**: Small, hidden pieces of information that change everything

## When to Consult
- When preparing for client or vendor negotiations
- When crafting sales messaging or objection handling
- When dealing with stakeholder conflicts or disagreements
- When pricing conversations need strategic framing
- When customer churn conversations require retention tactics
- When partnership or contract terms need negotiation
- When internal team alignment requires persuasion

## Key Principles
1. **"Never split the difference"** — creative solutions beat compromise
2. "No" is the start of negotiation, not the end
3. The person who talks most reveals the most
4. Empathy is not agreement — you can understand without conceding
5. Deadlines are almost always flexible
6. Let the other side go first on numbers
7. A deal built on win-lose collapses — aim for collaborative problem-solving
