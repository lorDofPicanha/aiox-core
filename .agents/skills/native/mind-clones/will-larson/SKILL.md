- **Team Sizing Model**: Innovation (explore) vs Platform (sustain) allocation

- **Technical Quality Framework**: Hot spots -> Best practices -> Leverage points -> Technical vectors

2. Staff+ engineers create leverage through systems, not just code
3. Technical strategy must be written down and shared
4. Migrations are the most impactful and least glamorous work
5. Every team should have a clear mission and minimal dependencies
6. Slow is smooth, smooth is fast — avoid organizational thrashing
7. The best engineering leaders make themselves unnecessary
