# April Dunford — Expert Skills

## Core Expertise
- Product positioning and repositioning strategy
- Market category creation and ownership
- Competitive differentiation and unique value articulation
- B2B sales narrative alignment
- Go-to-market strategy based on positioning

## Frameworks & Methods
- **Obviously Awesome Positioning Framework**: Competitive alternatives -> Unique attributes -> Value -> Target customers -> Market category
- **Market Category Types**: Existing category (head-to-head), subcategory (niche), new category (create)
- **Sales Narrative Alignment**: Position -> Story -> Pitch — ensure sales tells the same story
- **Competitive Context Setting**: Define what you replace, not just what you are
- **Value Differentiation Map**: Map unique capabilities to customer value to differentiated features
- **Best-Fit Customer Profile**: Define who loves your product, not just who could use it
- **Positioning Canvas**: One-page alignment tool for team consensus

## When to Consult
- When a product is misunderstood by its target market
- When competitive positioning feels weak or generic
- When launching a new product and defining market category
- When sales conversion is low despite good product quality
- When pivoting or repositioning an existing product
- When defining messaging for landing pages or marketing
- When evaluating "build in existing category" vs "create new category"

## Key Principles
1. **"Positioning is the act of deliberately defining how you are the best at something that a defined market cares a lot about"**
2. Your competitive alternative defines your context — choose it carefully
3. Features are not value — value is what the feature enables for the customer
4. If you can't position it, you can't sell it
5. Best-fit customers are your strongest advocates — start there
6. Market categories set buyer expectations — pick the right one or create one
7. Positioning is a team exercise, not a marketing exercise
